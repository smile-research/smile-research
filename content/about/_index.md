## SMILE Research Group

SMILE is a machine learning research group at Silesian University of Technology. Our research focuses on machine learning, evolutionary algorithms, deep learning, and the analysis of medical and satellite imagery.

---

## Group Head

![Jakub Nalepa](/images/jakub-nalepa.jpg)

**Jakub Robert Nalepa, D.Sc.**

Jakub Nalepa is a professor at Silesian University of Technology in the Department of Algorithms and Software at the Faculty of Automation, Electronics and Computer Science.

Born in 1987, he graduated from Silesian University of Technology in 2011, earned his PhD in Computer Science in 2016, and completed habilitation in Technical Computer Science and Telecommunications in 2021.

He has published in prestigious journals including *Neurocomputing* and *Soft Computing*, and presented at leading international conferences such as GECCO and IEEE ICIP. Notable achievements include the Witold Lipski Award (2017) and POLITYKA Scientific Awards in Technical Sciences (2020).
